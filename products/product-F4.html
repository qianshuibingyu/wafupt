Product Introduction

WF-F4 fingerprint & password handle lock uses aluminum-alloy unibody, stores 20 fingerprints and passwords, easily covering the needs of a whole apartment or small hotel. The new dynamic-password algorithm can generate 6-digit temporary codes, allowing visitors, cleaners or couriers to enter and auto-expire afterwards.

Phantom-password anti-peep allows random digits before and after the real code; left/right opening reversible for 35-55 mm doors, no drilling, no wiring, even girls can finish in 3 minutes. Type-C emergency port prevents power-off; OEM/ODM supported, black in stock.
