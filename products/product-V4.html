Product Introduction

Designed for thermal-break aluminum doors, the WAFU WF-V4 combines a smart handle design with multiple access methods and long or short handle options.

For Distributors & OEM Partners

  • A smart handle lock solution for thermal-break aluminum doors
  • Aluminum-alloy construction with long and short handle options
  • OEM/ODM customization can be discussed on request

Access & Convenience

  • Unlock by fingerprint, passcode, IC card, mechanical key or Tuya App
  • Multiple access methods support flexible daily use

Fit & Installation

  • For thermal-break aluminum doors with 35–80 mm door compatibility
  • Confirm door and lock-body compatibility before ordering
