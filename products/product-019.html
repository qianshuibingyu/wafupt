Product Introduction

WF-019 Invisible Remote Lock is engineered for cross-border export. Its zinc-alloy die-cast shell receives nano-grade plating for ≥10-level corrosion resistance. Silver/gold/blue-silver finishes × remote/Wi-Fi/Bluetooth/fingerprint keypad combinations meet different platform needs.

A built-in low-power RF chip delivers ≥12 months battery life and Tuya Smart compatibility for quick access to mainstream smart-home platforms.
