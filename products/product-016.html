Product Introduction

WF-016 Handle Lock is manufactured in our original Shenzhen factory. It uses a zinc-alloy die-cast unibody with anodized surface ≥ grade-8 anti-corrosion. The patented "zero-wire installation" structure allows direct replacement of traditional cylindrical/mortise locks—no drilling, no wiring—completed in 3 minutes, greatly reducing installation cost.

Built-in semiconductor fingerprint (50 fingerprints capacity), 6-10 digit dynamic password and Tuya App provide three unlock methods; battery ultra-long life ≥365 days, and Type-C emergency power is supported. Black in stock, OEM/ODM customizable, mass-exported to 60+ countries in Europe, North America and South-East Asia, firmly ranked in Amazon & AliExpress "handle lock" hot-search list, the cost-performance king for bulk procurement by Airbnb, apartments and school dormitories.
