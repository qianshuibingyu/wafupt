Product Introduction

WF-010 Hidden Anti-Theft Lock uses a zinc-alloy die-cast unibody with nano-grade plating for ≥10-level corrosion resistance. Installed indoors, it presents no keyhole and no data cable outside, eliminating technical lock-picking and maximizing security. Twelve ready-made combinations are available: silver/blue-gold/blue-silver × 4 remotes, Wi-Fi remote, Bluetooth or fingerprint keypad—mix and match the 5-in-1 unlock modes you need. OEM/ODM supported; drop-shipping to Amazon, eBay and AliExpress is worry-free.

A built-in low-power RF chip delivers ≥12 months battery life; the App can issue keys and check records remotely. Fully certified CE/FCC/RoHS, it has been exported in bulk to 60+ countries in Europe, America, South America and the Middle East, steadily ranking in Amazon Smart Lock TOP100 and becoming the evergreen choice for bulk procurement by apartments, hotels and offices.
