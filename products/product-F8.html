Product Introduction

WF-F8 fingerprint & password handle lock is the premium model made in our original Shenzhen factory. It uses aluminum-alloy unibody with anodized surface ≥ grade-8 anti-corrosion, weighing only 0.7 kg yet offering super-large capacity: 50 fingerprints and 100 passwords, easily covering the needs of a whole apartment or small hotel.

Phantom-password anti-peep allows random digits before and after the real code; patented direction-free clutch enables left/right opening change freely, no drilling, no wiring, even girls can finish in 3 minutes. 4 × AAA batteries last about 200 days; OEM/ODM supported, it is a hot bulk-purchase product for apartments and school dormitories.
