Product Introduction

WF-F3 fingerprint & password handle lock adopts high-quality aluminum-alloy unibody crafting, supports 20 fingerprints and large-capacity password storage, easily meeting the smart-lock needs of a whole apartment, small hotel or office. Innovative dynamic-password algorithm generates 6-digit temporary codes offline within a limited time, allowing visitors, cleaners or couriers to enter and auto-expire afterwards, safe and convenient.

Phantom-password anti-peep technology allows random digits before and after the real code to effectively prevent password leakage; left/right opening reversible design for 35-55 mm doors, no drilling, no wiring, even female users can finish installation in 3 minutes. Type-C emergency port avoids power-off anxiety; in-stock supply, OEM/ODM customizable.
