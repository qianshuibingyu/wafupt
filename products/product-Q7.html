Product Introduction

WF-Q7 Smart Ball Knob Lock uses aluminum-alloy material with modern minimalist design, suitable for home, apartment, office, hotel and school dormitory. It supports multiple unlock methods including fingerprint, password, App remote unlock, card and key, providing high security and convenience.

Available in electroplated silver and baking-paint black, weight about 750 g. OEM/ODM customization is supported to meet different customer needs. Fully certified CE/RCC/RoHS, suitable for cross-border export, mainly sold to Europe, South America, South-East Asia, North America and Middle East.
