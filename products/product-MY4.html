Product Introduction

WF-MY4 3D Visual Peephole Lock is WAFU’s flagship new product, integrating 3D structured-light face recognition and a 4.3-inch indoor big screen for a clear view of the outside. Aviation-grade aluminum-alloy + acrylic body in black/champagne-gold fits both modern minimalist and neo-Chinese décor. Unlock methods: 3D face (50-100 users), fingerprint, password, IC card—200 users total—App remote and key 6-in-1; 0.5s fast recognition, suitable for all ages.

Peephole adopts HDR night vision for clear images in the dark; pressing the doorbell auto-captures photos/videos and pushes them to your phone in real time. Built-in DC7.4V rechargeable lithium battery, ≥500 charge cycles, Type-C emergency port prevents power-off. Standard 6068 lock body, left/right universal, OEM/ODM supported, mass-exported to 60+ countries in Europe, South America, South-East Asia, North America and Middle East, the first choice for high-end apartments, villas and hotels.
