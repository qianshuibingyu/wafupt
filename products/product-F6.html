Product Introduction

WF-F6 fingerprint handle lock uses aluminum-alloy unibody, 30% lighter than traditional zinc-alloy locks, saving freight on long-distance shipping. Built-in FPC semiconductor fingerprint sensor supports 50 fingerprints with ≥99% recognition rate and ≤0.5s unlock; Tuya Bluetooth App enables one-click unlock and temporary password sharing without gateway for remote management.

Direction-free clutch design allows left/right opening change freely; no drilling, no wiring; battery ultra-long life about 240 days, Type-C emergency port prevents power-off. Black in stock, OEM/ODM supported, mass-exported to 50+ countries in North America, Middle East and South-East Asia, the cost-performance king for bulk procurement by apartment and hotel operators.
