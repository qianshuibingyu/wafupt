WAFU Signs Spain National Distributor: a global benchmark for China smart manufacturing

21 Oct 2025 WAFU News Center WAFU Spain distributor global certification

WAFU has formally signed an exclusive national-distributor agreement with Spain’s top lock distributor. From now on, WAFU’s full portfolio—home remote locks, smart locks, invisible locks, electronic locks and smart invisible remote anti-theft locks—will flow continuously into Spain, Portugal and North-African markets, marking another milestone in WAFU’s global expansion.

1. Why Spain?

  • CE & RoHS in one go: the entire range passed the EU’s latest mechanical-safety and environmental directives, cutting the test cycle by 30 % compared with the industry average.
  • Validated under harsh climates: the lock body ran for 26 consecutive weeks in 40 °C Málaga heat and Bilbao salt-spray with zero failures, earning an “A+ reliability” rating from the local engineers’ association.
  • Culture-friendly design: a patented “invisible mounting plate” allows 3-minute retrofitting on traditional Spanish wooden doors or modern thermally-broken aluminium doors without damaging the door leaf.

2. Distributor model—from “selling products” to “building the brand together”

WAFU HQ provides:

  • EU stock hub: 3,000 complete units kept in the Spanish warehouse, slashing lead times.
  • Sales rebate + annual market-development fund, guaranteeing channel margin ≥ 38 %.

3. World-recognised quality—global certificates at a glance

To date WAFU has obtained:

  • Europe: CE, RoHS, RCC, REACH, EN 15684 burglary resistance
  • North America: FCC, UL 1034, ANSI/BHMA A156.2 Grade 1
  • Latin America: NOM, ANVISA exemption
  • Middle East: SASO, SABER, GCC Gulf-six unified approval
  • Asia-Pacific: RCM, KC, PSB, BIS, CCC

Exported to 62 countries, MTBF ≥ 100,000 cycles—far exceeding the 60,000-cycle industry norm.

4. Next step: from Spain into Latin America

In 2026 WAFU will launch simultaneously in Mexico, Colombia and Chile, targeting one million units annual sales in Spanish-speaking markets within three years, bringing China-smart-manufactured security and convenience to more families worldwide.
