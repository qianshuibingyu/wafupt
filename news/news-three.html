WAFU Invisible Remote Lock Wins Overseas Clients—On-Site Orders Flood In

22 Nov 2025 WAFU News Center WAFU invisible remote lock overseas orders

WAFU has once again entered the spotlight—several overseas delegations flew to headquarters at the same time to experience and secure the first batch of newly released invisible remote locks. With the unique selling point of “security you can’t see”, the WAFU invisible series continues to heat up overseas, and clients signed on-the-spot bulk orders exceeding 3,000 units, adding another vivid chapter to China smart manufacturing.

1. “Invisible” design, visible market

  • Fully hidden installation: the lock body is completely concealed inside the door, delivering a minimalist look that instantly upgrades home aesthetics; clients jokingly call it “the stealth fighter of locks”.
  • One-click remote open: standard RF remote penetrates 80 m, even through walls and doors; clients tested unlocking across three villa floors on site and the response was enthusiastic.
  • Fits 99 % of doors: whether European vintage wooden doors, Middle-East metal copper doors or South-East-Asia composite panel doors, retrofit is completed in 3 minutes without extra holes; clients praise: “Time efficiency is crucial for us.”

2. Experience equals order—“fall in love within five seconds”

To let clients fully feel the product charm, the WAFU team set up five interactive stations:

  • Silence test: repeated open/close inside a sound-insulated room, noise ≤ 35 dB; clients commented “quieter than turning a page”.
  • Anti-pry challenge: crowbars, wrenches and other tools were provided; clients tried for five minutes and the lock body remained undamaged, winning applause.
  • Sea-water spray: continuous 168-hour salt-spray simulating coastal corrosion; no rust spots, suitable for bulk deployment in port cities.
  • Extreme temperature: after -40 °C ~ +85 °C thermal shock, the lock still responds in seconds; clients joked “it could work on polar expeditions”.
  • Battery life: 20 operations per day, battery lasts 18 months; low-battery auto reminder and power-bank emergency supply earned praise for “full marks on details”.

3. On-site signing—“taking the production line home”

After the experience, several clients actively asked for additional clauses:

  • Full-container priority: clients voluntarily covered full-container freight just to jump the production queue and guarantee Christmas-season shelf stock.
  • Joint after-sales: WAFU provides 2 % free spare parts + online video technical training; clients committed to building a local service centre to achieve 24-hour on-site response.
  • Co-branding: clients bring their own social-media resources and will co-produce short videos with the WAFU marketing team, targeting over 10 million views within three months.

4. Client voice—“I want the same one when I get home”

“I posted the unlocking video in my family group; within ten minutes all my relatives were asking where to buy. This invisible design is so cool—it completely overturns my perception of locks.”

5. Future outlook—invisible security as the global standard

With this bulk order confirmed, WAFU has launched the “Global Million Invisible Locks” programme: by the end of 2026 two additional fully automatic lines will be added, capacity will rise by 50 %, and three major service centres will be built simultaneously in Europe, Latin America and the Middle East. The target is to exceed one million invisible remote-lock sales annually within three years, letting “invisible security” guard every corner of the world.
