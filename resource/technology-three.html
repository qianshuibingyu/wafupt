WF-019 Invisible Security Lock Deep Dive: Zinc-Alloy Build & Multi-Mode Smart Entry

28 Oct 2025 WAFU Technical Center WAFU invisible lock zinc-alloy build multi-mode entry

WF-019 invisible security lock is cast from premium zinc alloy and combines modern minimalist design to deliver discreet yet powerful protection for homes, apartments, offices and hotels. It supports remote control, Tuya Wi-Fi remote unlock, Bluetooth unlock and fingerprint & PIN keypad options, covering every user’s individual needs.

1. Zinc-alloy strength & durability

WF-019 uses premium zinc alloy as the main material, offering outstanding physical properties:

  • High strength & wear resistance: high hardness and strong impact resistance, effectively preventing forced entry
  • Excellent corrosion resistance: special surface treatment adapts to all climates and gives long service life
  • Precision die-casting: one-piece moulding ensures tight tolerances and smooth operation
  • Beautiful & durable: available in silver, gold and blue-silver, retaining their look for years

Zinc alloy provides high structural strength plus good machinability, enabling the complex geometry required for invisible mounting.

2. Invisible design & space optimisation

WF-019 is completely hidden after installation, delivering unique space advantages:

  • Maximum concealment: the lock body is fully embedded inside the door leaf; no hardware is visible from either side
  • Space saving: no external protrusions keep the doorway clean and elegant
  • Superior anti-theft performance: the hidden mount defeats both technical picking and brute-force attacks
  • Wide adaptability: suits wooden, metal or composite doors and installs flexibly

Invisible design boosts security while preserving the architectural aesthetic—perfect for modern minimalist décor.

3. Multi-mode smart entry system

WF-019 offers rich configuration choices for every user type:

  • Basic remote set: includes 2–4 RF remotes, simple and convenient
  • Wi-Fi remote unlock: Tuya Wi-Fi module for mobile APP unlock and real-time status monitoring
  • Bluetooth smart entry: direct phone-Bluetooth control, safe and reliable
  • Fingerprint & PIN keypad: combines fingerprint reader and code entry for multiple credential options

Three colours—silver, gold and blue-silver—are available, each with multiple configuration bundles for true customisation.

4. Application scenarios & technical advantages

WF-019 is engineered for multi-scene security:

  • Residential: invisible look preserves home aesthetics; remote unlock lets family members enter easily
  • Apartment rental: temporary-PIN function helps landlords manage short-term tenants
  • Office security: tiered authority management secures office zones
  • Hotel environment: can interface with hotel management systems to boost efficiency

The modern minimalist styling blends into any décor and upgrades overall space quality.

5. Cross-border e-commerce strengths & global market layout

As a cross-border exclusive, WF-019 offers strong international competitiveness:

  • Multi-platform support: fully compatible with eBay, Amazon, Wish, AliExpress, Lazada, etc.
  • Key-market coverage: mainly shipped to developed regions—Europe, North & South America
  • Brand-authorisation service: overseas distributors receive brand licence to increase market trust
  • Custom production: OEM/ODM available for special regional requirements

Rigorous quality testing ensures compliance with international safety standards for reliable global performance.

6. Installation, use & after-sales

WF-019 focuses on user experience and hassle-free installation:

  • Professional install guide: detailed multi-language manual and video tutorial supplied
  • Modular design: core parts can be replaced individually, cutting maintenance cost
  • Optimised battery life: low-power design extends battery service life
  • Quality assurance: strict QC system ensures product reliability

Note: comprehensive after-sales technical support is provided to guarantee prompt help whenever needed.
