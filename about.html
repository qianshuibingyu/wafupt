Our History Partner with Us

Huafu Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd.

Founded in 2013, Huafu Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd. is dedicated to the R&D and innovation of smart door locks. With cutting-edge technology and a professional team, we provide customers with comprehensive OEM/ODM smart-lock solutions.

  • High-security design
  • Easy installation
  • Multiple unlocking methods

WAFU Smart Locks

We are committed to delivering high-quality smart door-lock solutions for homes, apartments, hotels and more, while offering global customers professional OEM/ODM custom manufacturing services. From ID design and function development to branded packaging, we provide full-process personalization to help clients build differentiated competitive advantages.

All products pass multiple international certifications to meet global standards. Whether sold under our own brand or through OEM/ODM partnerships, our solutions now serve 8000+ customers worldwide, helping them create smarter, safer living environments.

Beyond premium products, we provide full technical support and service. From concept design to after-sales care, our customer-service team stays with you every step, ensuring every requirement is met efficiently and professionally.
