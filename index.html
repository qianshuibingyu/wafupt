Company Profile

Shenzhen Huafu Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd. has 12 years of experience in smart-lock R&D and manufacturing and is a national high-tech enterprise specializing in one-stop OEM/ODM smart lock solutions.

Our Invisible remote locks and smart explosion-proof series integrating core technologies such as wireless remote control, biometric recognition, and hold multiple patents to provide superior security. The product line covers Invisible Remote Lock, handle Locks, Face-Plate Locks, Peephole Camera Locks and Ball Knob Locks to meet security needs in homes, hotels, apartments and offices.

WAFU have strong custom development and agile manufacturing capabilities and provide cost-effective smart-lock OEM/ODM services.WAFU brand leads sales in Spain and Portugal, and we cooperate deeply with nearly 30 global e-commerce platforms such as Amazon, eBay and AliExpress, ranking top in production and sales in the industry. We are committed to becoming a trusted global partner for smart-security solutions with leading technology, reliable quality and competitive prices.
